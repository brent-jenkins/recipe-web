Why Zayvori

Everything you need, nothing you don't

No social pressure, no algorithm, no noise. Just a calm, personal space for the recipes you love.

Save from Anywhere

Clip recipes from any website, social media, or type them in manually. If it's a recipe, Zayvori can store it.

Organize Your Way

Create custom collections — weeknight dinners, holiday baking, meal prep — and find any recipe in seconds.

Your Recipes, Your Device

Everything lives on your phone — nothing is uploaded, and nothing is shared. When you're ready for a new device, back up your collection to a single file and bring it with you.

Add Personal Notes

Jot down tweaks you made, substitutions that worked, or reminders for next time. Your notes live with the recipe, private to you.

Your Own Photos

Snap a photo of what you made and attach it to the recipe. When you import from a link, we bring in the text — never someone else's picture.

Offline Ready

No signal? No problem. Zayvori saves recipes locally so your collection is always accessible — in the kitchen, at the shops, or somewhere with patchy connection.

Our Philosophy

A quiet space, just for you

Your recipe box was never meant to be a performance. Zayvori is a private, personal space — nothing you save is visible to anyone but you, stored only on your own device.

No accounts, no profiles, nothing to sign up for. Just your recipes, kept the way you like them, for everyday cooking, family meals, or something new to try.

Not a social network

There are no followers, no public profiles, no comment sections, and no one else ever sees what you save. No pressure to perform — just recipes to enjoy.

No algorithm, no noise

Your collection is your own. Nobody sees your choices, nobody ranks them, nothing is optimized for engagement. No feeling of being "not enough" — just cooking.
