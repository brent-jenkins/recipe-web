Why Damson Kitchen

Everything you need, nothing you don't

No social pressure, no algorithm, no noise. Just a calm, personal space for the recipes you love — and the practical tools to actually cook them.

Save from Anywhere

Import from any recipe site, or paste a caption from Instagram or TikTok. Damson Kitchen pulls out the ingredients and method — and sorts them into sections where the recipe has them.

Cook Mode

One step at a time, in large text you can read at arm's length with floury hands. Your screen stays awake while you cook, and the ingredients are always one tap away.

Plan Your Week

Drop recipes onto the days you'll cook them, Monday to Sunday. Add an occasion for a birthday lunch, and say how many you're feeding — the amounts follow.

A Shopping List That Adds Up

Built from the meals you've actually planned, grouped by aisle so you're not criss-crossing the shop. Two recipes needing flour become one line, not two.

Organize Your Way

Group recipes into collections — weeknight dinners, holiday baking, meal prep. Heart the ones you come back to, and filter by breakfast, dessert or whatever you're after.

Cooking for Four, or Fourteen

Change the number of servings and every amount rescales with it — in fractions you'd actually use, not awkward decimals. The recipe itself always keeps its original amounts.

Your Recipes, Your Device

Everything lives on your phone — nothing is uploaded, and nothing is shared. When you move to a new device, back the whole collection up to a single file and bring it with you.

Your Own Photos and Notes

Snap what you made and attach it. Jot down the tweak that worked or the substitution to remember. When you import from a link we bring in the text — never someone else's picture.

Pass One On

Send a recipe you've written to someone by message or email — as plain text they can read without installing anything. Imported recipes send the original link instead, so the credit stays with whoever wrote it.

Our Philosophy

A quiet space, just for you

Your recipe box was never meant to be a performance. Damson Kitchen is a private, personal space — nothing you save is visible to anyone but you, stored only on your own device.

No accounts, no profiles, nothing to sign up for. Just your recipes, kept the way you like them, for everyday cooking, family meals, or something new to try.

Not a social network

There are no followers, no public profiles, no comment sections, and no one else ever sees what you save. No pressure to perform — just recipes to enjoy.

No algorithm, no noise

Your collection is your own. Nobody sees your choices, nobody ranks them, nothing is optimized for engagement. No feeling of being "not enough" — just cooking.
